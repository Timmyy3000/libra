# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. Be useful. Don't be a jackass.

More concretely:
- assume good intent, but don't tolerate harassment
- critique ideas and code, not people
- keep collaboration constructive
- respect maintainers' time and project scope

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive conduct.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or ban participants who ignore these rules.
